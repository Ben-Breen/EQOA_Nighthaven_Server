### TAP capture boundary — source evidence, runtime result still unknown

`CONFIRMED — reference`, scoped to official PCSX2 v2.8.2 source: [TAP sends through the common adapter handler before writing to the TAP device](https://github.com/PCSX2/pcsx2/blob/v2.8.2/pcsx2/DEV9/Win32/tap-win32.cpp#L575-L586). The [common handler intercepts UDP/53 addressed to the emulator's internal address](https://github.com/PCSX2/pcsx2/blob/v2.8.2/pcsx2/DEV9/net.cpp#L377-L416) independently of the DHCP-interception setting. Its [Windows DNS implementation can call `GetAddrInfoEx`](https://github.com/PCSX2/pcsx2/blob/v2.8.2/pcsx2/DEV9/InternalServers/DNS_Server.cpp#L266-L286). Handled DNS, and DHCP when interception is enabled, can therefore be absent from a TAP capture.
